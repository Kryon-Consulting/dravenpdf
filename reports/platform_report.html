01 · Executive summary

A single path from signal to action

Valio Guard puts posture, operational findings, attack-path context, asset inventory, and assurance evidence in connected workspaces.

The demo Dashboard makes the current risk position legible at a glance, then points investigators toward prioritized fixes. Findings adds volume, age, and SLA context; Exposure narrows work to reachable assets and paths; Inventory shows the underlying resources; Compliance tracks framework status and evidence.

02 · Dashboard

Risk posture with a clear next step

The landing view pairs a security verdict with reachability, remediation speed, and estate context.

What it communicates

The selected account is rated Critical with 1,097 critical or high risk resources, up 24 since the last scan. The view also shows 104 reachable crown jewels and a 42.4-day mean time to remediate.

How to use it

Use the risk-driver link and prioritized-action queue to move from the headline to a principal, resource, finding, or remediation. Preserve the account and date labels when sharing the numbers.

03 · Findings

Volume, age, and service-level pressure

The Trends & SLA view helps explain where remediation is slow and where critical findings remain open.

Operational signal

The page reports 11,294 open findings, including 1,573 critical or high and 6,569 overdue. The plotted services compare open-finding volume with remediation time.

Action

Start with overdue, high-severity groups and open the associated findings before assigning work. The regional list offers another way to find concentrations of aged debt.

03a · Findings chart

Compare risk volume with remediation speed

Each plotted service sits at the intersection of open findings and mean time to remediate. Bubble size represents critical and high severity volume.

How to read this chart

Services farther right have more open findings; services higher up take longer to remediate. The upper-right area is the clearest candidate for a focused investigation, with severity and overdue status checked in the underlying findings list.

03b · Findings chart

See where regional SLA debt concentrates

The regional panel pairs SLA compliance with critical-finding counts and aged volume.

Follow-up

Use the regional bars to select a queue, then confirm severity, age, and ownership in the underlying findings before assigning remediation. These percentages reflect the displayed demo scope and its partial coverage.

04 · Exposure & attack paths

Move from a finding to a reachable route

This workspace relates entry points, paths, targets, guardrails, and remediations.

The selected sandbox account shows six critical resources in its current exposure panel, while the panel shows zero internet-exposed resources and zero of zero crown jewels reachable from an internet-exposed entry point. The tabs below invite investigation of individual routes and ranked remediations. The account selector is central: changing scope changes the meaning of every count on the page.

Report-reading rule

Label both account scope and scan provenance on any exported screenshot. A zero on this sandbox view does not negate the company-wide or highest-risk-account signals elsewhere.

05 · Inventory

Trace the posture back to assets

The list provides searchable resources, account and region context, criticality, findings, and exposure.

The page shows a 3,197-resource daily rollup and a live list of 3,210 matching resources. Risk-ranked rows make it possible to open a specific identity or workload and inspect its findings. The difference between those two counts is a reason to retain their labels, not to silently reconcile them into one total.

06 · Compliance

Assurance status with evidence routes

Frameworks, evidence, audit readiness, breakdowns, and drift share one workspace.

The demo reports a 12% average compliance score, 45 evaluated frameworks, and 1,488 of 9,198 requirements passing. The page labels all 45 frameworks in the Critical (<60%) band. Use the framework and evidence tabs to validate which controls are driving that score before selecting remediation work.

07 · Recommended operating rhythm

Turn the view into a review cycle

A repeatable sequence keeps action tied to evidence and scope.

1
Confirm freshness and coverage.

Read the selected account, rollup date, scanned-account count, and stale-account note before interpreting any score.

2
Review the Dashboard's recommended fixes.

Open the linked paths and affected resources; record the expected reduction and owner.

3
Work finding debt by severity and age.

Use Findings' SLA and regional views to prioritize overdue critical and high items, then track their status.

4
Validate the asset and control context.

Trace selected findings through Inventory and Exposure; use Compliance evidence to confirm control-level impact.

5
Revisit after the next scan.

Compare the same scopes and grains, and note changes in coverage before describing movement.
